# Worker Quick Guide

Use Stryda on your phone to log your hours.

## Log your day

1. Sign in with the email and password your admin gave you.
2. On the **Dashboard**, find today's job. Press **Clock In** when you start and **Clock Out** when you finish.
3. Or press **Log My Time** to enter hours yourself: pick the job and cost code, the date, and the hours.
4. Check **My Entries** to see the status of each entry.

## Week at a glance

The Dashboard shows your hours for the week. Tap a day to start a new entry for that date.

## What the app will not accept

- More than 16 hours in one entry
- A date in the future
- A date more than 14 days ago
- The same entry twice in a few seconds (wait a moment and try again if you meant to add more hours)

You will see a message that says what to fix.

## Fixing a mistake

- If the entry is still **submitted**, you can edit it.
- If it was **rejected** or flagged, fix it and send it again.
- If it is **approved**, it is locked. Press **Request Correction** and your supervisor will review it.

## Materials

Use **Material Requests** to ask for materials for a job.
